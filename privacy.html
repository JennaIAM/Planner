Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Planner is a personal notebook app. It is built so that your notes stay with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Planner does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your notes are kept

Everything you write or draw (entries, doodles, trackers, to-dos, goals and notes) is stored only on your own device. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Backups

If you choose “Save backup”, the app creates a file and opens your phone’s share menu. Where that file goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email) is up to you, and it is handled by the service you pick.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device by the app itself. No server is involved and nothing about your reminders leaves your phone.

Children

Planner does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
